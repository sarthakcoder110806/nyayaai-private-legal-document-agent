SAMPLE RENTAL AGREEMENT

This is a fictional agreement created for software testing.

1. PARTIES

The landlord is Alex Sharma.
The tenant is Jordan Verma.

2. MONTHLY RENT

The tenant shall pay monthly rent of INR 12,000.
Rent must be paid on or before the 5th day of each month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT

The tenant shall pay a security deposit of INR 24,000.

4. PROPERTY MAINTENANCE

The tenant is responsible for keeping the premises clean.

5. TERMINATION

Either party may terminate this agreement by providing
30 days' written notice.

6. LATE PAYMENT

This sample agreement does not specify a late-payment penalty.
